# DuitRia 🏦🎲

**DuitRia** is an offline multiplayer board game for up to 4 players. Players roll dice, move around the board, buy properties, pay rent or taxes, and face random events. The goal is to become the wealthiest player by the end of the game.

---

## 🎯 Game Overview

* **Players**: Up to 4
* **Starting Money**: RM15 million per player
* **Objective**: Become the richest player through strategic property transactions.

---

## 🗺️ Game Board

**Grid**: 11 x 11 (40 tiles total)

### 🧱 Tile Types

* **Simple Tiles (2 Marks)**

  * Land on unowned property: Buy it or skip.
  * Land on your own property: Build houses to enhance it.

* **Fate (1 Mark)**

  * Triggers a random event card when landed on.

* **Tax (0.5 Mark)**

  * Deducts RM2 million from your account.

* **Jail (0.5 Mark)**

  * Go to Jail space or via "Go to Jail" card.
  * No RM2M salary collected when jailed.
  * Ends your turn.
  * “Just Visiting” if landed normally.
  * Exit Jail: roll doubles (once) or pay RM250K.
  * Still allowed to collect rent and sell property while in Jail.

* **Free Parking**

  * No reward or penalty – just a resting space.

---

## 🎮 Player Mechanics (0.5 Mark)

* Up to 4 players take turns.
* First player is chosen by highest dice roll.

---

## 💸 Key Gameplay Rules

### GO (1 Mark)

* Passing or landing on GO gives RM2M salary (once per round).

### Buying Property (1 Mark)

* Buy unowned property at listed price.
* Receive Title Deed.
* Optional to skip purchase.

### Paying Rent (0.5 Mark)

* Land on another’s property: pay rent as per Title Deed.
* Rent increases with houses/hotels.

### Houses (0.5 Mark)

* Round 1: Cannot buy yet.
* Round 2: Can buy unowned properties.
* Round 3+: Can build houses (max 4 per property).
* House prices listed on Title Deeds.
* Owning full color group = double rent for unimproved properties.

### Selling Property (0.5 Mark)

* Sell to Creator at 50% value.
* Selling order: Hotels → Houses → Properties.

### Bankruptcy (0.5 Mark)

* If debts can't be paid:

  * Owe another player: give all assets (half value).
  * Owe Creator: give all assets.
  * Bankrupt players retire.
  * Last player standing wins.

---

## 🔄 Miscellaneous (0.5 Mark)

* Only Creator may loan money.
* No player-to-player loans allowed.
